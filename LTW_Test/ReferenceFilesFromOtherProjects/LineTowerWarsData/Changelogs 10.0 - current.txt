Version 12.0a | 2026-03-22

GENERAL
New Season!

With this version Season 12 begins.
The top 100 Players from the Season 11 Leaderboard have been added to the in-game Hall of Champions and Discord channel.

Player Data is not compatible from previous Seasons and your settings have to be reapplied in-game. Unfortunately a technical limitation as of now.


NBA Command (Notify Being Attacked, “-nba”)

This command state is now saved between games (enabled by default).
GAMEPLAY
Game Modes

Ranked:
The number of players required changed from 4 to 3.

The end game has been redesigned with the goal of separating the early to late game from Sudden Death (“end game”) with the focus on reducing complexity.


We now have two distinct sessions in the game:

Early to Late Game: Tier 1 to Tier 3 (00:00 to 24:59 game time)
This session the main focus is to prepare the maze, gaining income and to steal lives from other players
Sudden Death: Tier 4 (25:00+ game time)
This session the main focus is to use any advantage gained from previous session together with game knowledge and skill in order to win the game
Everything necessary to win the game should be available in Tier 4 only

Income Cap & Convert Lives into Income Cap

This mechanic has been removed.

The income cap mechanic was complex and the UI didn’t support it well enough.



Sudden Death

Starting time changed from 26:30 to 25:00 game time.
Creep damage taken modifier changed from 90% to 100%.
Creep damage taken modifier reduction changed from 0.5%/min to 1.6%/min.
Tier 4 now unlocks with Sudden Death.
Tier 1, 2 and 3 can no longer be used during Sudden Death.
Players with less than 1,000,000 income have their income increased to 1,000,000 when Sudden Death begins.

GAMEPLAY
Catch-up Gold

The amount of gold given when a player receives a new attacker with higher income compared to the previous attacker changed from x1 to x1.5.

Example:

Your old attacker had 100,000 income and your new attacker has 500,000 income. The difference between these two is 400,000 income, which means your old attacker was most likely not sending much to you and spending most of their gold defensively. In order for you to catch-up on gold from lost bounty you’re given a one-time gold bonus equal to their income difference multiplied, in this case 400,000 x 1.5 = 600,000 gold.

Health Regeneration

Base health regeneration is set to 0 for all creeps and their maximum health has been (in most cases) increased to compensate. The amount depends on which tier the creeps belong in, as mazes vary in length and strength during different stages.
Tier 1: Health Regeneration * 10
Tier 2: Health Regeneration * 20
Tier 3: Health Regeneration * 30
Tier 4: Health Regeneration * 60

The health regeneration of creeps was a hidden mechanic, but has never been fully considered and had semi-random values.


(Health Regeneration of towers is unchanged at 1.5% of max hp/sec.)



Auras

Known to cause stuttering performance issues when many units are affected by auras at the same time. As an attempt to improve this issue the AoE of auras above 700 AoE has been lowered to 700 AoE.
Towers and Creeps affected are detailed in their changes below.


(War Beasts War Stance attack damage bonus aura being the one exception, keeping its 2,000 AoE.)


Ancient Aura has been removed and is replaced with Regen Aura:
The ancient aura had very little to no use after a creep was fully slowed and was pretty much a limited Endurance Aura.
Regen Aura increases the health regeneration of affected creeps by x per sec, countering low damage sources, such as non-upgraded towers, splash, etc. The more creeps affected by the aura, the more value given with the total health regeneration contributed. The aura is allowed to be strong as creeps now lack health regeneration by default and given its limited AoE.
Creeps affected are detailed in creep changes below.


With health regeneration removed from creeps, Regen Aura allows for new strategic sends and counters.


Slow Mechanic

The base duration of slow changed from 3 to 4 seconds.
Slow resistant duration changes from 1.5 to 2 seconds.
Slow amount is now visible on a new info panel when a creep is selected.

These changes should make slow feel a bit more reliable and consistent.

The info panel can be expanded in the future.


Income

Player income is now capped at 4,000,000.
Can be extended with a new purchase option in Tier 4, details below.

Sudden Death

Now begins at 26:00 game time (up from 25:30 game time).
No longer freezes income.
Damage taken modifier no longer adjusts based on player income.
Damage taken modifier changed to a constant 0.16% per tick (=1%/minute).

Income and Sudden Death changes force players to better balance their sends for income and stealing lives in order to get the late game advantage. With previous versions income had greatly more value compared to player lives and hopefully these changes make it closer to 50/50.


The goal is still to maximize income, but up to the new cap and then focus on maze or attempt to maximize lives, instead of passive income spam up to Sudden Death start.

General Changes

Timers have been adjusted for all creeps and now follows a pattern:
All creeps in a tier unlocks over a 5 minute timer.
Creeps are unlocked in pairs every 1 minute.
Tiers have a set start time:
Tier 1: 00:00 game time
Tier 2: 07:00 game time
Tier 3: 14:00 game time
Tier 4: 21:00 game time
Initial stock once unlocked changed from 1 to half of maximum.
Note: Tier 4 unchanged as they are infinite stock.
Stock replenish interval changed from 5 to 8 seconds for regular creeps and from 10 to 16 for boss creeps.

The goal with the timer changes is to provide a clear gameflow structure and slightly reduce the focus on income spamming weaker creeps.
