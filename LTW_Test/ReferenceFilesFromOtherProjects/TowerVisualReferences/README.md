# What is in these screenshots

Screenshots of the Warcraft III **Line Tower Wars 12.4a** towers, taken out of the source
game. They are REFERENCE, not assets: nothing in the build loads one, and they are here to be
looked at while placeholder art is made.

## How to read a tech sheet

Every `*Tech.png` is one element, laid out the same way. **Two rows of five towers, and both
rows read LEFT TO RIGHT from the cheapest tower to the most expensive:**

| Column | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Price rung | 200g | 800g | 4,000g | 10,000g | 30,000g |
| Tier name | *(base pair)* | *(base pair)* | Lesser | Greater | Ultimate |

- the **top row is that element's PATH (1)**, the bottom row its **PATH (2)**
- **columns 1 and 2 are the same two towers in both rows.** An element's 200g and 800g towers
  are shared by both of its paths, so each sheet draws them twice - once at the head of each
  row. They are the only towers on a sheet that appear more than once
- the two rows only diverge at column 3, which is where the path silhouette arrives. That is
  the split the game itself makes, and it is why the sheets are worth reading a row at a time
  rather than a column at a time

`ElementalBase200g.png` is the odd one out and is a single tower: the **Elemental Core**, the
200g technology base tower every element is morphed out of. It carries no element yet, which
is why it is a bare worked pad with one red rune on it and no colour of its own.

A green circle around a tower in one of these shots is the source game's own selection ring,
and a blue bar over one is its mana. Neither is part of the tower.

## The sheets

Names below are this project's, not the source game's - the source renames a tower at every
upgrade and this project does not. `unit_data.md` 2.4 has the naming scheme, the full
Lesser/Greater/Ultimate names, and a column giving the original Warcraft III name of every one
of them. The `.tres` files under `Resources/UnitStats/Towers` are the authority for anything
numeric; nothing here is.
